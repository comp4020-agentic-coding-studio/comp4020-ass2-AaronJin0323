# Involution 101

## Geertz's Rice Paddies

---

## The word

**Involution** (n.): a system absorbing more and more labour, more and more
elaboration — without producing more output.

Not stagnation. Not growth. A third thing.

---

## Clifford Geertz, Java, 1963

Wet-rice (sawah) agriculture under colonial pressure: population rose, land
didn't. The response wasn't mechanisation or collapse.

It was **finer and finer terracing, more and more hands per hectare** — yield
per hectare crept up, yield per hour of labour fell.
