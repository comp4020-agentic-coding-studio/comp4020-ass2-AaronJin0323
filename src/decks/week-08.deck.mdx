## The KPI regime, formalised

Scholarship, subjected to the same instrumentation as any other output
metric:

- citation count, as a proxy for impact
- impact, as a proxy for quality
- quality, as a proxy for whatever the citation count was already measuring

A closed loop, audited by itself, in triplicate.

## The self-audit (run this on your own workload)

1. Take last year's version of whatever you're doing.
2. Count the steps it took.
3. Take this year's version of the same thing.
4. Count the steps again.
5. If step 4 is bigger than step 2, and the outcome is the same outcome,
   you have found an instance. Cite the mechanism, not the feeling.
