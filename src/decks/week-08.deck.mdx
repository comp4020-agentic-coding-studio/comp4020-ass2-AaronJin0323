## The KPI regime, formalised

Scholarship, subjected to the same instrumentation as any other output
metric:

- citation count, as a proxy for impact
- impact, as a proxy for quality
- quality, as a proxy for whatever the citation count was already measuring

A closed loop, audited by itself, in triplicate.

## The self-audit (run this on your own workload)

1. Take last year's version of whatever you're doing.
2. Count the steps it took.
3. Take this year's version of the same thing.
4. Count the steps again.
5. If step 4 is bigger than step 2, and the outcome is the same outcome,
   you have found an instance. Cite the mechanism, not the feeling.

## Where this syllabus sits in its own audit

Applying the instrument above, to this document, honestly. Words per
lecture, weeks 1 through 12:

79 · 58 · **103** · 63 · 72 · 55 · 70 · 69 · 67 · 65 · 55 · **98**

The expected curve was a rise into week 8 and a decline after it. The
measured curve peaks at week 3 and again at week 12 — the two plainest
weeks on the syllabus. Week 8, the local maximum this slide was written to
report, is a local dip.

One count fell while every other rose: the teaching team, three at the
start, one now. It fell because one person covering all three roles set
the baseline the other two were measured against — week 3's 卷王, as a
staffing outcome.

The escalation this course performs is real. It is in register, not in
length, and its nearest case is whoever is running the audit. Reported as
found: the instrument does not care which answer the author wanted.
