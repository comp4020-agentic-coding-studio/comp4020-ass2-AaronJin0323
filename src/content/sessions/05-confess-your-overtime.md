## Before the session

Track one week of your own hours against this course, or against a job.
Tag each hour as "impact" (produced a verifiable result) or "visibility"
(was observed in the act of working).

## In the session

Anonymous tally on the board: total hours logged versus hours anyone can
attribute a result to. The delta is this week's key finding.

## Afterwards

This is the last session before the two-week break. Carry your key finding
into week 6 — Assignment 1 is due that week, and presenteeism remains a
high-confidence candidate instance if you haven't finalised one yet.
