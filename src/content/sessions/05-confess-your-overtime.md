## Before the session

Track one week of your own hours against this course, or against a job.
Mark each hour "produced something" or "was seen doing something."

## In the session

Anonymous tally on the board: total hours claimed versus hours anyone can
point to a result for. The gap is the week's real subject.

## Afterwards

This is the last session before the two-week break. Bring the gap you found
back with you in week 6 — Assignment 1 is due that week, and presenteeism is
a strong candidate instance if you haven't settled on one yet.
