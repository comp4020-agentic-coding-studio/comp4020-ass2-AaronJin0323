## Before the session

Read the lecture. Come with one real instance — a habit, a hobby, a group
chat, a job — where you've been putting in more for the same or less.
Nothing invented for the occasion: Assignment 1, due in week 6, asks you to
go back to whatever you name today.

## In the session

A round of one-minute pitches: the instance, since when, what you've tried
that didn't help. The class sorts the pitches into "involution," "just
hard," and "actually improving" — the sorting is the exercise, not the
pitching.

## Afterwards

Keep whatever instance survives the sorting. It's the raw material for
Assignment 1.
