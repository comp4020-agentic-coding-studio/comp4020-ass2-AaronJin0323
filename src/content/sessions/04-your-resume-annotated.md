## Before the session

Bring a resume, printed or on a laptop. Classify each line as "informs" or
"competes," and for every competing line, name the specific pool of other
candidates it is positioned against.

## In the session

Swap annotated resumes for a peer competitive review. The room audits each
other's classifications — a line one person flags "informs" is frequently
reclassified by someone else as "competes."

## Afterwards

Retain the annotated version as a reference artefact. It functions as a
useful benchmark when Assignment 1 asks you to justify why your own
instance constitutes involution rather than ordinary effort.
