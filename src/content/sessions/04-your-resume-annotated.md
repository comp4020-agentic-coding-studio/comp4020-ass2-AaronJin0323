## Before the session

Bring a resume, printed or on a laptop. Mark each line either "informs" or
"competes," and for the competing lines, name the other applicants it's
addressed to.

## In the session

Swap annotated resumes. The room checks each other's marks — a line one
person calls "informs" is often the line someone else immediately flags as
"competes."

## Afterwards

Keep the annotated version. It's a useful contrast when Assignment 1 asks
you to justify why your own instance counts as involution rather than
ordinary effort.
