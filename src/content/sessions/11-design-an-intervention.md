## Before the session

Draft one intervention into a system covered this semester — workplace
overtime, credentialism, engagement farming, admissions, housing, any of
them. A page is enough; specificity beats scope.

## In the session

Studio format: work the draft against the room's feedback, focused on
whether it changes the incentive structure or just displaces the pain.
Leave with a version you'd actually submit.

## Afterwards

This draft is the direct starting point for the final project — bring the
room's feedback to it, don't discard it.
