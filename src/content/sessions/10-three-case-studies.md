## Before the session

Read into one of: K-pop trainee systems, standardised-test admissions
races, or housing markets as positional goods. Identify the mechanism you
think makes it involution — not a summary of the domain, an argument.

## In the session

Pairs compare their two domains mechanism against mechanism, in front of
the room. The room's job is to find exactly where the comparison strains.

## Afterwards

The strongest comparison, and its strongest rebuttal, are written up as a
shared reference for the final project.
