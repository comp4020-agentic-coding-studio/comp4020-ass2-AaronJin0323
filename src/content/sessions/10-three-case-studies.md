## Before the session

Read up on one of: K-pop trainee systems, standardised-test admissions
arms races, or housing markets as positional goods. Bring the mechanism you
think makes it involution, not just a summary of the domain.

## In the session

Pairs compare their two domains directly, mechanism against mechanism, in
front of the room. The room's job is to find the point where the metaphor
strains.

## Afterwards

The strongest comparison, and its strongest counter-argument, both get
written up as a shared reference for the final project.
