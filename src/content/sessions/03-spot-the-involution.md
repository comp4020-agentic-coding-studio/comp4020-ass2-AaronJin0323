## Before the session

Pick a system you're actually in — a course, a team, a hobby community — and
work out whether effort in it is rising, and whether outcomes are rising to
match.

## In the session

Present the system in two minutes. The room cross-examines: is this
involution, or just a system that's genuinely getting harder to win?

## Afterwards

Systems that survive cross-examination as "genuinely involuted" go on the
running class list — this semester's evidence base, built collectively.
