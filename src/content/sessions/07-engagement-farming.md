## Before the session

Pick a public account or feed you can track posting frequency and
engagement for over a few weeks. Bring numbers, not an impression.

## In the session

Present the diagnosis and defend it against the room's counter-evidence —
someone will have a reading of the same numbers that isn't involution.

## Afterwards

The strongest diagnoses, and the strongest rebuttals, both go on the running
class list from week 3.
