## Before the session

Pick a public account or feed and instrument it: track posting cadence
and engagement over several weeks. Bring a dataset, not an impression.

## In the session

Pitch the diagnosis and defend it against the room's counter-read on the
same funnel — someone will have an alternative attribution that isn't
involution.

## Afterwards

The strongest diagnoses, and the strongest counter-attributions, both get
added to the running class list from week 3.
