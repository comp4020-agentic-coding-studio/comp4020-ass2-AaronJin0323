## Before the session

Reread this course's own spec pages. Come with a verdict: where, if
anywhere, does Advanced Involution's own workload rise without its learning
rising to match?

## In the session

The room argues the verdict out loud, including with the people who teach
it. Nothing here changes a mark; it's the closest the semester gets to
turning the method on the thing teaching it.

## Afterwards

Whatever survives this session as a genuine cut candidate gets named in
class, on the record, whether or not it ever actually gets cut.
