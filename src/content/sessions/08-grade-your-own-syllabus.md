## Before the session

Reread this course's own spec pages as an internal stakeholder would.
Arrive with a findings statement: where, if anywhere, does Advanced
Involution's own workload trend upward without commensurate evidence of
impact on learning outcomes.

## In the session

The room presents findings to the people who deliver the course, in the
manner of an accreditation panel. Nothing here changes a mark; it is the
closest the semester comes to the method auditing the institution that
teaches it.

## Afterwards

Any component identified as a genuine discontinuation candidate is
recorded on the record, whether or not it is subsequently discontinued.
