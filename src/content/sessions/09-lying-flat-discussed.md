## Before the session

Pick one of 躺平 (lying flat), quiet quitting, or FIRE. Bring one case —
a person, a post, a movement — and be ready to defend it as a genuine exit,
not a rebrand of giving up.

## In the session

Three camps argue their strategy against the other two, then against the
room's hardest question: does this actually break the loop, or just opt one
person out of it while the loop keeps running for everyone else?

## Afterwards

Whichever strategy survives the room's cross-examination best gets carried
into week 11 as a candidate shape for an intervention.
