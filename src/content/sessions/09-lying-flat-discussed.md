## Before the session

Pick one: lying flat, quiet quitting, or FIRE. Bring one real case. Be
ready to defend it as an exit, not a rebrand of giving up.

## In the session

Three camps argue their exit against the other two. Then one question:
does this break the loop, or just let one person step outside it while it
keeps running for everyone else?

## Afterwards

Whatever holds up best carries into week 11 as a shape for an
intervention.
