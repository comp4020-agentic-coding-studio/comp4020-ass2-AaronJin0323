## The brief

> Take one zero-sum system from this semester and design an intervention
> that would actually shift its equilibrium, not just redistribute its pain.

Build on the draft from week 11's studio and the room's feedback from the
final crit. A realistic scope for the time available is one system, one
mechanism, one intervention — argued precisely — rather than a survey of
everything covered this semester.

## What you submit

The intervention design itself (written, or another format if it fits the
proposal better), plus a short justification connecting it explicitly to
the mechanism it targets and naming who bears the cost if it works.
