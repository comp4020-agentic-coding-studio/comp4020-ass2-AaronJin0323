Designs the syllabus, delivers every lecture, runs every seminar, and marks
every submission, alone. Other courses split this across a convenor, a
lecturer, and a tutor; Advanced Involution's entire teaching team is one
person doing all three roles for the pay and hours of one — which is either
a staffing failure or the single most rigorous auto-ethnography of
involution this course will produce all semester.

No office hours roster to check, no "ask the tutor" versus "ask the
convenor" — every question goes to the same inbox, answered by the same
person who wrote the question that prompted it.
