Designed Advanced Involution's twelve-week arc on the theory that the best
way to teach a concept about escalating effort is to build a syllabus that
does it in front of you. Runs the odd-numbered weeks' seminars and holds the
final say on which "instance of involution" submissions actually qualify.

Office hours run Tuesdays; email first for anything that can't wait for the
weekly session, and expect a straight answer about whether your case study
is really involution or just a bad week.
