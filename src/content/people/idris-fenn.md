Ran the even-numbered weeks' crits, from the résumé annotation in week 4
through to the final crit in week 12, and brought up the Red Queen effect
more than strictly necessary — including, on the record, at the meeting
where the convening role was first described as coverable. Kept his crits
to their scheduled two hours while the coverage arrangement beside him
grew to absorb everything else. By the time the review reached his
position the comparison had already been made, and it was not a comparison
about teaching: one colleague was visibly doing three roles, and he was
doing one.

No longer runs sessions. Kept on the People page as the clearest evidence
the course has that naming a mechanism out loud, early, repeatedly, and at
the correct meeting does not exempt you from it.
