Two pieces of graded work, worth 40 and 60 percent, marked two different
ways. One is scored against weighted sub-criteria that state exactly how
much each part is worth. The other is judged holistically, as a whole,
against the brief. Between them, that's the full range of what a marking
scheme can promise: itemise the precision, or declare up front that
itemising it wouldn't help. Read both criteria sections below and decide
which one you trust more before you've written a word for either.
